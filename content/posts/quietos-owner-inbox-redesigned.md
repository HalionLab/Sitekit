QuietOS is built on a simple promise: digital employees do the work in the background, and the owner only sees finished results waiting for approval. The Owner Inbox is where that promise lands — and the first version of it didn't earn the name "quiet."

## What changed

- **One queue, not five.** Drafts, scheduled jobs, and completed workflows used to live in separate tabs. Now everything that needs a human decision arrives in a single approval queue, newest first.
- **Approve or edit in place.** The most common action — "yes, send it" — is one click. The second most common — "almost, tweak this line" — no longer bounces you to a separate editor.
- **Everything else got quieter.** Status chatter, progress states, and logs moved behind a detail view. If it doesn't need a decision, it doesn't get a notification.

## Why it matters

The test for every QuietOS screen is whether an owner can clear it between two customer appointments. The redesigned inbox is the first version that passes.

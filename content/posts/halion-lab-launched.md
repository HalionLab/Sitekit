Halion Lab is live. It's a workshop, not a startup landing page: a place where we explore AI, test software ideas, and build tools that remove friction from real work — with the process documented as it happens.

## What we're building

- **Mosaic** — AI that sounds like you. A voice signature engine that helps AI write the way you do.
- **QuietOS** — digital employees that quietly handle work. Rooms that run workflows and return finished work for approval.
- **Small Business Trend** — research and education for AI adoption. Practical insights for owners who don't have time for hype.

## Why in public

Building in public keeps us honest in two directions. It forces every experiment to produce something showable — a screen, a note, a lesson — instead of a vague sense of progress. And it lets the people these tools are for see the reasoning, not just the launch: what we tried, what broke, and what we changed our minds about.

## The philosophy

Three rules shape everything here:

1. **Useful over impressive.** Technology should remove burden, not create it.
2. **Systems over hacks.** Durable processes beat clever demos.
3. **Augment, not automate.** Automation runs quietly in the background so people work with more leverage — not less control.

This build log is the front door. Every experiment, lesson, and product decision lands here as we go. Build quietly. Ship often.
